Editor's Pick

Morikami Gardens & Museum

Six Japanese gardens in chronological sequence, tucked behind sugar cane fields. Bring patience; the koi will outlast you.

Open 10–5 16 min west $16

Wakodahatchee Wetlands

Three-quarters of a mile of boardwalk over restored marsh. Alligators by 8 AM, herons all day, anhingas drying their wings like sails.

Open Sunrise–Sunset 9 min west Free

Cornell Art Museum

Inside Old School Square's 1913 schoolhouse. Four floors of rotating contemporary art and a courtyard that smells of jasmine.

Open 11–4 On the Ave $10

Sandoway Discovery Center

A 1930s beach house become children's nature museum. The 3 PM shark feeding is small theatre with educational consequences.

Open 10–4 Tue–Sat 3 min east $6

The Pineapple Grove Murals

A self-guided mile of large-scale wall art, NE 2nd Avenue. Best in late afternoon, when the sun rakes across the brick.

Open Always Walking Free

The Pier at Anchor Park

Two blocks of paved promenade, one historic boathouse, the Atlantic on three sides. Lock the bike to the south rail and go barefoot.

Open 5 AM–10 PM 4 min east Free
